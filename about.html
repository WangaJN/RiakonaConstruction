About Us

WHO ARE WE

Riakona is a proudly South African construction company built on a foundation of integrity, craftsmanship, and client satisfaction. With years of industry experience, we've earned a reputation for delivering solid, innovative building solutions that stand the test of time.

WHAT DO WE DO

We specialise in new builds, renovations, tiling, ceilings, plastering, and paving. Serving residential, commercial, and public sector clients. Every project is treated with precision, professionalism, and attention to detail.

OUR MISSION

To build lasting structures and long-term relationships. At Riakona, we don't just build buildings, we build trust through consistent quality, transparent communication, and reliable service.

OUR VISION

To become a leading force in South Africa's construction industry by setting new benchmarks in quality, safety, and customer satisfaction.

OUR VALUES

Integrity: We keep our promises and operate with honesty.
Excellence: We deliver superior workmanship on every project.
Safety: We create a safe environment for our workers and clients.
Teamwork: We believe success is built together.
Innovation: We apply smart solutions and modern techniques to enhance project outcomes.
