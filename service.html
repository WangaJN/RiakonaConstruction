Our Service

At Riakona construction, we offer a wide range of professional construction services to meet both residential and commercial needs. Our work is defined by quality, safety, and client satisfaction.

Bricklaying

Involves skilled workers in the company laying bricks, blocks, and stones with mortar to create walls, foundations, and other masonry structures according to building plan.

Plastering

Application of a cement, lime, or gypsum mixture to rough surfaces to create a smooth, durable, and aesthetically pleasing finish on walls and ceilings.

Plumbing

Installing, maintaining, and repairing the water supply, drainage, and waste systems for new buildings and renovations.

Electrification

Implementation of electrical systems for power and control within sites and buildings.

Waterproofing

Creating an impermeable barrier using specialized materials and methods to prevent water from penetrating a building's structure, safeguarding its integrity, preventing leaks, and protecting occupants and finishes from water damage and deterioration.

Extension and Renovation of House

Improving or expanding existing buildings to enhance functionality, comfort, and modern appeal while maintaining structural integrity.

Office Partitioning

Designing and installing partitions to create functional and organized office spaces that improve privacy and productivity.

Project Management

Overseeing the entire construction process from start to finish, including project planning, coordinating drawings and design, organizing resources, managing teams, and ensuring smooth handover upon completion.

Paving

Laying durable and visually appealing surfaces using concrete, bricks, or stone for driveways, walkways, and outdoor areas.
