FAQ

Common Questions
What type of construction project do you handle?

We handle residential, commercial, and industrial projects including new builds, renovations, tiling, ceilings, plastering, and paving.

Are your services available across South Africa?

Yes, we operate nationally and can mobilize teams for projects across different provinces.

How long will my project take?

Project timelines vary based on size and complexity. After an initial assessment, we'll provide a detailed schedule and keep you updated throughout.

Can you help with planning and design?

Yes. We collaborate with architects and engineers and can assist in the planning, design, and approval phases.

What materials do you use?

We use high-quality, locally sourced materials that meet industry standards to ensure durability and long-lasting results.

How do I get started with Riakona?

Simply contact us via phone, email, or our website form. We’ll schedule a consultation and guide you through the next steps.
